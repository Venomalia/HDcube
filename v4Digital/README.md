# HDcube 4 Digital
HDcubeDigital is a universal model for most modern image formats like Png, Gif, Jpg, DDS and more.

It can handle a variety of artifacts and filtering modes, producing sharper or softer images as desired.

Similar to HDcube 4 it consists of 2 modes!
Model A is finer and softer, best for DDS, Gif and dithering;
Model B is rougher and sharper, best for PNG & JPG;
